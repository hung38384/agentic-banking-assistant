# MB Visa Classic Credit Card Product Guide

The MB Visa Classic Credit Card is available to applicants aged 20 years or older with minimum monthly income of 8,000,000 VND and at least 6 months of employment history or stable verified income. Approval is subject to CIC credit bureau review, internal risk policy, fraud screening, and human underwriting review.

The annual fee is 299,000 VND and can be waived when annual card spending reaches 60,000,000 VND. Late payment fee is 4% of the overdue minimum payment amount, with a minimum fee of 100,000 VND. Cash advance fee is 4% of the advanced amount, with a minimum fee of 100,000 VND.

Customers earn reward points on eligible retail spend. Reward points do not apply to cash advances, fees, interest, gambling transactions, refunded transactions, quasi-cash transactions, or transactions flagged by risk monitoring.

Credit limit changes, card replacement after suspected compromise, chargeback support, hardship requests, and credit card application submission require authenticated customer consent and human review. The assistant may explain documented card features and eligibility rules, but must not guarantee approval, recommend this card as the best option, or provide personalized financial advice.
