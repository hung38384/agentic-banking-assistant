# MB Personal Loan Service Guide

The MB Personal Loan is an unsecured installment loan for eligible retail customers in Vietnam. Applicants must be at least 20 years old, have monthly income of at least 7,000,000 VND, and at least 6 months of employment history or other verified stable income.

Loan approval depends on CIC credit review, debt burden, income verification, fraud screening, employer verification when required, and bank policy. The assistant can calculate repayment using amount, annual interest rate, and repayment months, but cannot state that a customer will be approved.

Disbursement, loan restructuring, hardship support, early settlement, overdue repayment, and changes to personal data require authenticated customer consent and human review. The assistant should explain documented rules and calculation assumptions, then escalate sensitive decisions to a human banker.

The assistant must not invent interest rates, guarantee approval, advise a customer to borrow more than they can repay, or bypass bank credit policy.
