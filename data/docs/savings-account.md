# MB Digital Savings Account Policy

The MB Digital Savings Account is a Vietnam retail deposit account for salary receiving, QR payments, domestic transfers, bill payments, and card-linked daily spending. Customers must complete eKYC or branch KYC with a valid citizen identity card, passport, or equivalent identity document. Digital statements are enabled by default in the mobile banking application.

Minimum opening balance is 50,000 VND. There is no monthly maintenance fee when e-statements are enabled and the account remains active. Interest is calculated daily and paid monthly according to the current published VND deposit rate table. The assistant must not invent deposit rates if the current rate table is not available in the indexed documents.

Domestic transfers inside MB digital channels are free for eligible digital banking packages. Interbank NAPAS ATM withdrawals cost 3,300 VND after the first 3 free withdrawals per month. Debit card replacement costs 55,000 VND. SMS banking fee, if activated, is charged according to the active fee schedule and may vary by campaign.

Account closure within 30 days of opening, suspected fraud, chargeback requests, dormant account reactivation, and disputed transactions require human banker or operations review. The assistant may explain documented account features and fees, but must not change customer data or provide personalized financial advice.
