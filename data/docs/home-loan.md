# MB Home Loan Policy

The MB Home Loan supports owner-occupied residential property purchases, apartment purchases, house renovation, and refinancing from another bank when allowed by policy. Applicants must be at least 20 years old, have monthly income of at least 15,000,000 VND, and have at least 12 months of employment or stable business income.

Loan amount, tenor, pricing, collateral margin, and approval depend on collateral valuation, debt service ratio, CIC credit history, repayment behavior, and bank approval. The collateral appraisal fee is 1,500,000 VND for standard residential property files. Early repayment within the first 3 years may incur a fee equal to 1% to 3% of the prepaid principal amount depending on the signed credit contract; after year 3 this fee may be waived according to product policy.

Repayment estimates should use the documented amortization formula and clearly state that the result is an estimate before final approval, insurance, taxes, notarization, collateral registration, and other third-party fees. Sensitive actions such as product recommendation, loan application submission, credit decisioning, or collateral valuation dispute require human banker review.

The assistant must not guarantee loan approval, quote unpublished preferential interest rates, override underwriting conditions, or infer a customer's full creditworthiness from chat messages alone.
